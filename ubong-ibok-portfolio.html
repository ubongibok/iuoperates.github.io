Frequently asked

Before you reach out

Isn't covering seven functions too much for one person?

Most clients only need two or three functions active at once, the value is having one accountable person who already understands how your finance, product, and admin work connect, instead of three contractors who don't talk to each other.

Which function should we start with?

Whichever one is causing the most pain right now usually payroll or bookkeeping. We start narrow, prove the handoff works, then expand into adjacent functions.

Do you work full-time or on a fractional basis?

Fractional by default — a set number of hours or deliverables per week with the option to move to a dedicated full-time retainer as scope grows.

Do you work with Nigerian public sector payroll requirements?

Yes, IPPIS processing, CONHESS/CONTISS grading, GIFMIS and TSA reporting, and IPSAS-compliant financial statements are core to my background.

How do you keep our financial data secure?

Access stays within your approved systems, and every engagement is bound by a signed confidentiality agreement before onboarding begins.
